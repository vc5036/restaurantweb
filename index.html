Our Story

A flame passed
through three generations.

Braise & Ember began in 1987 as a single hearth in my grandfather's courtyard, where he cooked for neighbors on cast iron and coal. What he understood — that fire has moods, and a cook must learn to listen to them — is still the first lesson every chef in our kitchen receives.

Today, that same hearth sits at the center of our dining room. Everything that leaves it is dry-aged, hand-cut, or slow-rendered by people who remember why we started. We are not chasing trends. We are keeping a flame lit.
